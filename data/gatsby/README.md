# The Great Gatsby, in lines

`lines.txt` holds line-sized pieces of F. Scott Fitzgerald's *The Great
Gatsby* (1925), one per line, for the mishear page's "random line": each a
sentence of 5 to 14 words, or a clause of that length from a longer one
(split at commas, semicolons, colons and dashes), every word of it in
CMUdict, without its double quotes or the punctuation around it.

The novel is in the public domain (in the United States since 2021). The
text is Project Gutenberg's ebook #64317, without its header, license or
contents; `crates/mishear/scripts/gatsby_lines.py` regenerates the file
from it.
