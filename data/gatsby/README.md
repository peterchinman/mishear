# The Great Gatsby, in lines

`lines.txt` holds sentences of F. Scott Fitzgerald's *The Great Gatsby*
(1925), one per line, for the mishear page's "random line": narration, not
dialogue (no quotation marks, not inside a quotation), 5 to 14 words long,
every word readable by mishear (in CMUdict, or derived from it by
mime-reader's rules: "Gatsby's", "pointlessly", "sleeplessness"), without
the final period. There are 365.

The novel is in the public domain (in the United States since 2021). The
text is Project Gutenberg's ebook #64317, without its header, license or
contents; `scripts/gatsby_lines.py` regenerates the file
from it.
