# Proverbial phrases

`lines.txt` holds the proverbial phrases of Wikipedia's
[List of proverbial phrases](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_proverbial_phrases),
one per line, for the mishear page's "random line": each 3 to 20 words,
without digits, every word in CMUdict, without its citations, notes and
attributions (an optional part in parentheses is kept, "A friend in need is
a friend indeed"; of "a/b" the first is kept). There are 690.

The phrases are traditional sayings; the list is Wikipedia's, under
[CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/), and this
file, adapted from it, is shared under the same license. Fetched on
2026-11-14; `scripts/proverb_lines.py` regenerates it from the
page's wikitext.
